Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06

Summary

AI School does not have accounts and does not collect personal data. Your progress stays on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not ask for your name, email or any other personal information, and it has no ads or analytics.

What is stored on your phone

Your language, kids/adults choice, theme, finished lessons, quiz results and notes are saved only on your device. We cannot see them.

Internet connections

The app connects to the internet only to: (1) download new lessons from our website on GitHub Pages; (2) download the optional AI tutor model from Hugging Face, only when you tap download; (3) handle Pro subscriptions through Google Play. These services may see technical data such as your IP address, according to their own privacy policies. We do not receive this data.

AI tutor

The AI tutor runs entirely on your phone. Your questions and answers are not sent anywhere.

Payments

Pro subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details; the app only receives whether a subscription is active.

Children

The kids version collects no data, has no ads and no purchases. Some lessons mention AI tools that have age limits (for example 18+); in those cases we recommend practicing with a parent using the parent's account.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings. All saved data is deleted.

Not official

AI School is an independent app and is not affiliated with Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or other companies whose tools it teaches.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page. Questions: aischool.support@gmail.com
